# Apple 2.1 回覆紀錄（已送出）

使用者於 2026-09-20 確認「協會及合作人員專用、管理員開帳號、全部免費」。已於同日 21:33（台灣時間）在 App Store Connect 回覆 Apple，頁面訊息數由 1 增為 2，並顯示下列完整回覆。這是補充資訊，並非版本已重新送審或正式上架。

## 實際送出的英文回覆

Thank you for your review. This app supports student record management, daily attendance, classroom performance records, and growth reports. Here are the answers to your questions:

1. Yes. Access is restricted to authorized staff and partner personnel of 黃絲帶愛網關懷協會 (the Yellow Ribbon association).

2. The app is for the association and its authorized partner personnel supporting the association’s work. It is not an open service where any company can become a client or register to use the app.

3. There are no student-management features intended for the general public. An existing authorized account is required, and the app has no public account-registration flow.

4. Association administrators create accounts for authorized personnel. Eligible users obtain access through an administrator; users cannot create their own accounts in the app.

5. The app, accounts, and all features are free. There is no paid content, no charge to open an account, and no charge to access any feature.

The app is designed for iPad in landscape orientation, and availability is limited to Taiwan.

Thank you for helping us determine the appropriate distribution option.

## 發布方式

已確認僅供協會與合作人員使用，可考慮 Apple 的 Unlisted App：正式透過 App Store 連結安裝，不出現在搜尋結果。它仍需正式審查，連結本身不能取代登入權限。此選項尚未申請，也尚未選定；本次回覆未宣稱已完成申請。

Apple 要求先讓 App 處於可正式發布狀態並提交 App Review，在審查備註說明預計採 Unlisted，再提出申請。不得現在把 App 切成需要 Apple 商務／校務組織 ID 的私人自訂發布，當作 Unlisted 的同義設定。

依據：[Apple Unlisted app distribution](https://developer.apple.com/support/unlisted-app-distribution/)。
